3. Flatheads vs. IEMs:
   - Non-sealing flathead earbuds must be classified as "Earbuds (Wired)".

   - Any non-detachable / hardwired earphone or headphone must be assigned connector "Fixed Cable" (e.g., Moondrop Quarks DSP, Tanya DSP Type-C).
   - Detachable IEMs with Type-C DSP cables must be labeled by their shell connector socket (e.g., "2-pin" or "MMCX").
